## Merhaba

NURCAN DENLI BAYIR received the M.D. degree from the Faculty of Medicine, Zonguldak Bülent Ecevit University, Zonguldak, Türkiye, in 2019.

From 2019 to 2021, she worked as a General Practitioner at Sorgun State Hospital, Yozgat, Türkiye.

Since 2022, she has been a Research Assistant (M.D., Ph.D. candidate) with the Department of Forensic Medicine, Ankara Bilkent City Hospital, Ankara, Türkiye, where she is expected to complete her Ph.D. in 2027.

She is concurrently pursuing the M.Sc. degree in Software Engineering at Hacettepe University, Ankara, Türkiye (2025–2027).

She also holds expert witness certificates in Forensic Informatics, Forensic Psychology, Forensic Document Examination, and Forensic Ballistics.

Her current research interests include computer vision, machine learning, deep learning, explainable artificial intelligence, Internet of Things (IoT), Arduino-based embedded systems, genetic databases, and data mining.
